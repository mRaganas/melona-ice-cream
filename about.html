About Melona Ice Cream

Delight in Every Scoop!

Welcome to Melona Ice Cream, a place for people who love sweet and refreshing treats. We offer different ice cream flavors that customers can enjoy with their family and friends.

From fruity Strawberry Swirl to creamy Vanilla Cloud and Filipino-inspired Ube Delight, we want every scoop to bring happiness and enjoyment to our customers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide delicious and enjoyable ice cream with different flavors at affordable prices. We want to make every customer's experience sweet, fun, and memorable.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a favorite ice cream destination known for our flavorful treats, friendly service, and commitment to customer satisfaction.

Why Choose Melona Ice Cream?

  • A variety of delicious ice cream flavors
  • Affordable prices for customers
  • A sweet treat for family and friends
  • A colorful and enjoyable ice cream experience
